In 2010 census of the city, the weight of the individuals in a small were found to be the following

< 50 kg - 20% , 50-75 - 30%, >75- 50%

In 2020 weight of n =500 individulas were sampled and below are the results

<50 - 140, 50-75 - 160, >75 - 200

Using apla= 0.05 would you conclude the population differences of weights has changed in last 10 years?

expected
<50 0.2*500 =100
50-75 = 0.3*500 = 150
0.5*500 = 250


1) null hypothesis - the data meets the expectation
alternate - the data does not meet the expectation

2) alpa = 0.05.   c.i =95%

dof = n-1 = 3 categories -1 = 2

decision boundary - critical value from chisquare table = 5.991

if chi square test is greater than 5.99 reject the null hypothesis else we fail to reject the null hypothesis

(140-100)**2/100 + (160 - 150)**2/150 +(200-250)**2/250
= 26.66 is greater than 5.99 we reject the null hypothesis
