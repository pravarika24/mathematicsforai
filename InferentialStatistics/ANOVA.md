Analysis of variance(ANOVA)

ANOVA is a statistical method used to compare the means of 2 or more groups

ANOVA 

1) Factors(variable)
2) levels

ex: medicine -  factor
5mg.       10mg.     15mg.     20mg (levels)
mode of payments - factor
google pay.     phonepe.      IMPS.   NEFT - levels



Assumptions of ANOVA

1) normality of sampling distribution of mean
The distribution of sample mean is normally distribution
2) Absence of outliers
outlying score need to removed from the dataset
3) homogenity of variance
population variance in different levels of each independent variable are equal
4) Samples are independent and randomly selected


Types of ANOVA(3 types)
1) one way anova - one factor with atleast 2 levels 1 of these levels are independent

Doctor wants to test a new medicatio to decrease headache 
they split the participants in 3 conditions[10mg, 20mg, 30mg] - levels
Doctor ask the participants to rate the headache[1-10]

2) repeated measures ANOVA. - one factor with atleast two levels are dependent 

Running - factor 

Day1    day2   day3 - with the same person - levels are dependent

3) Factorial ANOVA - two or more factors (each of which with atleast two levels, levels can be independent or dependent)

Running - factor 

day1 day2 day3

gender as other factor
