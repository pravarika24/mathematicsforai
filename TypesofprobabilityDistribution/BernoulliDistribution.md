Bernoulli Distribution

The Bernoulli distribution is the simplest discrete probability distribution(Probability Mass function). It represents the probability distribution of a random variable that has exactly two possible outcomes: success(with probability p) and failure(with probability 1-p). It is used to model binary outcomes, such as a coin flip or yes/no question

1) Discrete Random Variable (PMF)

2) Outcomes are binary

Example: 1) Tossing a coin

P(h) = 0.5

p(t) = 1-0.5 = 0.5

2) Whether the person will pass/fail

P(x=pass) = 0.4
p(x=fail) = 1 - 0.4 = 0.6
