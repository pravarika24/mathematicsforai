Poisson Distribution

In probability theory and statistics the poison distribution is a discrete probability distribution that expresses the probability of a give number of events occuring in a fixed interval of time if events occur with a known constant mean rate and independently of the time since the last event

1) Discrete random variable(PMF)

2) It describes number of events occuring in a fixed time intervals

example - number of people visiting hospital every hour

number of people visiting bank every hour

lambda = 3 - expected number of events occuring at every time interval

PMF 

P(x=5) - probability of person visiting at 5th hour

p(x=5) = e(power of - lamba)(lambda to the power of x)/x factorial

e**(-3)*(3)(5)/5! = 0.101 = 10.1%
