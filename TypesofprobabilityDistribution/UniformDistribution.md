Continous Uniform Distribution[continous random variable]

In probability theory and statistics, the continous uniform distributions ore rectangular distributions are a family of symmetric probability distributions. Such a distribution describes an experiment where there is an arbitary outcome that lies between certain bounds. The bounds are defined by the parameters, a and b which are the minimum and maximum values

if x belongs to a,b
the PDF  = 1/b-a or 0 
CDF = 0 for x<a
1 for x>b

mean = 1/2(a+b)
median = 1/2(a+b)
variance= 1/12(b-a)**2

Example: The number of candies sold daily at a shop is uniformly distrubed with a maximum of 40 candies and a max of 10

Probability of daily sales to fall between 15 and 30

Discrete Uniform Distribution
In probability theory and statistics the discrete uniform distribution is a symmetric probability distribution where in finite number of values are equally liked to be odserved. Everyone of n values has equal probability 1/n. Another way of saying "discrete uniform distribution" would be "a known, finite number of outcomes equally likely to happen"
