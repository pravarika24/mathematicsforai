5 Number summary
1) Minimum
2) 1st quartile
3) Median
4) 3rd quartile
5) maximum

example = 1,2,2,2,3,3,4,5,5,5,6,6,6,7,8,8,9,27(27 is outlier)

lower fence ----- higher fence

any thing below lower fence and anything after higher fence will be removed


lower fence q1- 1.5(IQR)
Higher fence q3 +1.5(IQR)

q1 = percentile/100 *(n+1)

25/100(19+1) = 5th position that is 3

q3 = 75/100(19+20) = 15th position = 7

IQR = Q3-Q1 = 7-3 = 4

Lower fence = 3 - 1.5(4) = -3
higher fence = 7 + 1.5(4) = 13

Anything below -3 and anything above 13 are outlier 

so now 1,2,2,2,3,3,4,5,5,5,6,6,6,7,8,8,9

min = 1
q1 = 3
q3 = 7
median = 5
maximum = 9
